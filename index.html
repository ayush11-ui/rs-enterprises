Our Services

Four simple services, all with trained and honest staff.

Security Guard सुरक्षा गार्ड

Trained security guards for offices, factories, shops, hospitals, colonies and homes. Guards handle entry control, checking, patrolling and safety.

Gunman बंदूकधारी

Licensed gunmen for VIP, cash, jewellery and high-value protection. Every gunman holds a valid gun licence and security training.

Housekeeping Staff सफाई कर्मचारी

Trained cleaning staff for offices, hospitals, hotels, schools and homes. Regular cleaning, floor care and sanitisation to keep every place neat.

Supervisor सुपरवाइज़र

Experienced supervisors to manage guard and housekeeping teams. They look after duty posts, attendance, uniforms, reports and discipline.
